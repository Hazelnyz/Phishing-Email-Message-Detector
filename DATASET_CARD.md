# Dataset card

Generated by `run_pipeline.py`; sizes below come from `evaluation/metrics.json`.

## Sources and use

- Email training corpus: [Phishing Email dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/subhajournal/phishingemails), listed in third-party records as LGPL-3.0; confirm the originating download terms before redistribution. Email body and label fields only; headers and source artifacts may remain.
- Independent email validation: [Zenodo record 13474746](https://zenodo.org/records/13474746), 2,000 labeled email texts; record does not specify a license. Used only after selection.
- URL corpus: [UCI PhiUSIIL](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/967/phiusiil+phishing+url+website+dataset), CC BY 4.0. Uses only raw URL and label; 1=legitimate, 0=phishing. No URL is fetched or resolved.
- SMS: skipped because the raw SMS folder contains no CSV.

## Limitations

Email source data can contain collection artifacts; template-family splitting and source-marker audits reduce but do not eliminate leakage. The handwritten hard set and domain cases are small, and should not be treated as estimates of production accuracy. URL lexical models cannot inspect page contents. Metrics and sample counts: see the generated `evaluation/metrics.json`.
